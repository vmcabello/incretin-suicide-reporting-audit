The independent count script verified the input/core hashes, unique complete
grid of 1008 records, correspondence of analytical states and numeric indicator,
28 separately randomized populations, 27 registry identifiers, and expected
inapplicability of all-cause mortality derivation to ideation and attempt.
All input files were read only. This is not a new independent human review of
scientific source classification and does not validate each source judgment.

Principal quantitative observation
Every applicable frozen cell is complete or unknown; there are no bounded
not-complete cells. Consequently all upper conditional identification limits
are 100%. The data establish confirmed documentary witnesses and unresolved
coverage. They do not establish a proportion of trials demonstrably incapable
of reconstruction, nor that most trials are not reconstructible. Unknowns must
remain in N; complete-case denominators would misleadingly report 100%.

Main presentation
1. Separate all four references, three components, and applicable modalities.
2. Present absolute direct-complete, derived-complete, unknown, bounded-negative
   counts before conditional percentages. Show NA explicitly, not as 0/28.
3. Reference-specific any-modality unions are secondary and count each trial
   population once. Never add modality positives, references, arm counts or
   person/event data. Within secondary unions, C_direct means at least one
   direct witness; C_derived is derived-only completeness, not all units having
   any derived witness. Explain overlapping direct and derived witnesses.
4. Report k/N to (k+u)/N as identification intervals conditional on this exact
   finite corpus, source snapshot and coding. No sampling confidence interval,
   p-value, effect meta-analysis, or causal source-layer interpretation is needed.
5. Later technical closure is an AI assessment using historical ratifications,
   not newly supplied human votes, new blinded validation, or new agreement data.

Defensible sensitivities
- Four references are alternative target definitions, not poolable replications.
- Withhold all-cause-mortality modality and recompute the secondary union from
  AE and structured assessments; retain in-scope unknowns and original N=28.
- Direct-witness-only: withhold complete-derived witnesses. Because the frozen
  file has no direct-only bounded-examination judgment, a previously complete
  derived-only cell becomes unknown, not negative. The outcome is sufficiency of
  the retained direct evidence, not a claim that no direct document exists.
- Show source/program strata (6 SCALE, 15 STEP, 7 SURMOUNT) and/or omit-one-
  program scenarios; label exploratory composition checks and do not interpret
  program differences as drug differences or independent drug-risk estimates.
- Optionally omit the four author-ratified population-amendment units (N=24),
  or the two diet-run-in populations (N=26). These are scope sensitivities,
  never claims that approved membership was invalid; retain full core as main.
- Keep SURMOUNT-OSA GPI1 and GPI2 as separately randomized primary units. If a
  registry-level sensitivity N=27 is desired, define 'all nested populations
  reconstructible': complete only when both OSA populations are complete; bounded
  noncomplete if any is bounded noncomplete; otherwise unknown. This changes
  the estimand to registry coverage. Do not collapse by any-positive and call it
  equivalent to trial-population completeness. No patient pooling is performed.

Important limitations
All judgments use a current cumulative documentary snapshot dated 11Nov2026.
They cannot reconstruct dated A/B1/B2/B3 layers without source admissibility
chronology. Historical 182/159 analyses remain a different corpus/date and must
not be treated as before/after effects of these changes. Source-safety and
on-treatment witnesses can differ in population, assignment and follow-up rules
across studies and across modalities. More positives in one reference do not
demonstrate a causal benefit of restricting follow-up or adding source types.
