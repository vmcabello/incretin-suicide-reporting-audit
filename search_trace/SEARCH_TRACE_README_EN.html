An individual search-update trail identifies each of the 2,558 saved representations, its source database and native record locator, the relevant update-screening card or reused prior-library decision, the recorded decisions and author-written rationale, and any documented trial-family and operational-unit linkage. The 745 update cards, directed reviews and later corrections are retained as separate stages. Identifier matching and recorded decision presence do not establish scientific closure or independent duplicate review.

Some prior-library explanations and final trial links remain unavailable or unestablished in this bounded export. These gaps are itemised rather than inferred from identifier matching. Sources retained only for citation tracking, excluded source-level reports and planning records do not necessarily require an admitted trial-unit link. The audit corpus remains non-exhaustive and exploratory.

All 745 initial cards have supplied returns; a route match does not certify scientific correctness, completion of full-text follow-up, or independent assessments.
