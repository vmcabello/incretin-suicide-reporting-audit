Interpretation

This is a partial inventory of what prior annotations recorded. Their directed and heterogeneous coverage cannot tell how much of the observed result is caused by the chosen reference rather than by public-reporting limitations. An independently coded source-based sample using explicit alternative populations and windows is needed for that question. The five feasibility cases are illustrative, post-hoc candidates; no numerical sensitivity was fitted.
